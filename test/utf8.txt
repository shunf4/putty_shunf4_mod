Overflowable symbols (half-width cell, glyph may spill into a blank
cell on the right).  These are half-width in the buffer so TUI
applications that assume width 1 don't misalign, but the full glyph is
drawn when the next cell is blank:

  Arrows:            ← → ↑ ↓ ↔ ↕ ↖ ↗ ↘ ↙
  Arrows + blank:    ← → ↑ ↓ ↔ ↕
  Roman numerals:    Ⅰ Ⅱ Ⅲ Ⅳ Ⅴ Ⅵ Ⅶ Ⅷ Ⅸ Ⅹ Ⅺ Ⅻ   ⅰ ⅱ ⅲ ⅳ ⅴ ⅵ ⅶ ⅷ ⅸ
  Enclosed digits:   ① ② ③ ④ ⑤ ⑥ ⑦ ⑧ ⑨ ⑩ ⑪ ⑫ ⑬ ⑭ ⑮ ⑯ ⑰ ⑱ ⑲ ⑳

  When the next cell is a real character (not blank), these should be
  SQUEEZED into the half-width cell instead of overflowing:
  ←X →X ①X ⑤X


Half-width default-text symbols whose glyph is wider than one cell.
U+26A0 (WARNING SIGN) is the canonical case kept in the overflow table;
rendered in colour in the emoji build, and overflowed when the right
cell is blank:
  Warning:   ⚠
  With blank: ⚠ ⚠ ⚠
  Squeezed against a character: ⚠X ⚠X ⚠X

  With VS15 (U+FE0E, force text style, monochrome; overflow follows the
  bare symbol — U+26A0 still overflows into a blank right cell):
  ⚠︎   ⚠︎X

  With VS16 (U+FE0F, force emoji presentation, colour):
  ⚠️


Emoji via U+FE0F (a half-width text-default symbol + VS16 -> colour
emoji presentation).  These OCCUPY ONE cell in the buffer (half-width,
so TUI apps that count columns aren't broken) and DRAW the full-width
colour glyph overflowing into a blank right cell when one is available
(GNOME Terminal / xterm / wezterm / Alacritty style; NOT Windows
Terminal, which occupies two cells).  When the right neighbour is not
blank the glyph is squeezed into its single cell.  VS16 never changes
the buffer width — overflow is purely a drawing concern:
  ❤️ ☺️ ☹️        (right cell blank -> overflow, draw 2 cells)
  ❤️X ☺️X ☹️X      (right cell solid -> squeeze into 1 cell)

Dedicated emoji (already East Asian Wide: occupy 2 cells, draw 2 cells).
U+2615 HOT BEVERAGE belongs here (it is Wide), not with the half-width
VS16 group above:
  💜 🙂 🙁 👋 👍 😀 ☕

A Wide emoji followed by VS16 stays Wide (2 cells) — VS16 must NOT
collapse a Wide emoji to half-width:
  😀️ 🙂️ ☕️
