Overflowable symbols (half-width cell, glyph may spill into a blank
cell on the right).  These are half-width in the buffer so TUI
applications that assume width 1 don't misalign, but the full glyph is
drawn when the next cell is blank:

  Arrows:            ← → ↑ ↓ ↔ ↕ ↖ ↗ ↘ ↙
  Arrows + blank:    ← → ↑ ↓ ↔ ↕
  Roman numerals:    Ⅰ Ⅱ Ⅲ Ⅳ Ⅴ Ⅵ Ⅶ Ⅷ Ⅸ Ⅹ Ⅺ Ⅻ   ⅰ ⅱ ⅲ ⅳ ⅴ ⅵ ⅶ ⅷ ⅸ
  Enclosed digits:   ① ② ③ ④ ⑤ ⑥ ⑦ ⑧ ⑨ ⑩ ⑪ ⑫ ⑬ ⑭ ⑮ ⑯ ⑰ ⑱ ⑲ ⑳
  Black circle:      ● ●

  When the next cell is a real character (not blank), these should be
  SQUEEZED into the half-width cell instead of overflowing:
  ←X →X ①X ⑤X


Half-width COLOUR symbols.  These occupy one cell and are drawn in colour
(via DirectWrite); because a colour symbol wants a full-width glyph, they
are ALSO in overflow_glyph_chars.h, so they overflow into a blank right
cell with OR without a variation selector:

  Bare (no VS, right blank -> colour + overflow):
  ☺ ❤ ☹ ⚠

  Bare (no VS, right solid -> colour + squeeze):
  ☺X ❤X ☹X ⚠X

  With VS16 (U+FE0F, force emoji/colour -> colour + overflow):
  ☺️ ❤️ ☹️ ⚠️

  With VS16 + solid right cell (colour + squeeze):
  ☺️X ❤️X ⚠️X

  With VS15 (U+FE0E, force text/monochrome -> MONO, but still overflows
  because the base is an overflow glyph):
  ☺︎ ⚠︎

  With VS15 + solid right cell (MONO + squeeze):
  ☺︎X ⚠︎X


Dedicated emoji (already East Asian Wide: occupy 2 cells, draw 2 cells,
NEVER overflow).  U+2615 HOT BEVERAGE, U+26A1 HIGH VOLTAGE and U+2705
WHITE HEAVY CHECK MARK belong here (they are Wide), not with the
half-width group above:
  😀 ☕ ⚡ ✅ 💜 🙁 👋 👍

A Wide emoji followed by VS16 stays Wide (2 cells) — VS16 must NOT
collapse a Wide emoji to half-width:
  😀️ ☕️

A Wide emoji followed by VS15 stays Wide but turns monochrome:
  ☕︎ ⚡︎

Emoji forced to colour from a text default (U+3299 ㊙ is outside the
broad emoji blocks and is added via the force-colour table).  U+3299 is
East Asian Wide, so it occupies 2 cells and does not overflow:
  ㊙ ㊙ ㊙
